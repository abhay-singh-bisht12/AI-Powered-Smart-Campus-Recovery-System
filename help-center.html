Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for common student queries.

How long does approval take?

Usually reports are reviewed by admin within 24 hours.

Where can I check my report status?

You can check status in the My Reports section after login.

Can I upload item photo?

Yes, while submitting a report you can upload a clear item photo.

What if I find someone’s item?

Submit a Found report with location, date, and contact details.
